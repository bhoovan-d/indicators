    Sources

      • Fuel. Metro petrol and diesel are IOC retail prices published by PPAC. Other cities come from goodreturns.in; their history before daily tracking began is reconstructed from monthly summaries and drawn dashed. CNG history is monthly.
      • Gold. IBJA benchmark rates, PM where published, else AM. They exclude 3% GST and making charges and are the same nationwide.
      • Thali. The cost of raw ingredients for one veg thali (rice, wheat, tur dal, onion, tomato, potato) at Agmarknet mandi modal prices. These are wholesale prices, not retail. Each city uses the median of its own district's markets, falling back to its state, then to all-India. Plate cost is shown as a 7-day average because a city's daily figure swings when only a few of its mandis report; the raw daily cost stays visible on the chart.
      • AQI. The CPCB daily bulletin: a 24-hour average city AQI as of 4 PM.
